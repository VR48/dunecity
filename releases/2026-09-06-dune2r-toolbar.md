# DuneCity 1.0.535 / Android 0.2.24

## Dune2R View Controls

- Move the Dune2R zoom and graphics-mode controls out of the crowded top bar.
- Place both controls in a dedicated row below the top bar and clear of the
  sidebar at low resolutions.
- Give the controls stable dimensions and spacing so the Dune2R/Classic label
  change cannot move or overlap either button.

This is a presentation-only change. It does not alter gameplay, save data,
multiplayer behavior, bots, or asset compatibility.
