# Dune City 1.0.642

Changes since 1.0.630:

- Rebalance Dune City income around the Micropolis tax formula, with a 2x multiplier for private residential, commercial and industrial zones. Government infrastructure is tax-exempt; the Palace remains an exception with residential and commercial income. Windtraps no longer supply industrial jobs. Light Factories and Silos supply low-density industry; Refineries, Heavy Factories, High Tech Factories and Repair Yards supply medium-density industry; House IX supplies high-density commerce. Starports retain their seaport role.
- Choose R/C/I construction from positive, normalized demand and existing or queued provision, so commercial preference and residential infill no longer starve industry. Zones use normal configured construction timing. QuantBot compares tax investment with marginal refinery capacity, maintains a continuing tax-income hedge and expands refinery capacity when the harvester fleet needs it.
- Prioritize QuantBot's first High Tech Factory and carryall before additional Heavy Factories when prerequisites and placement allow. Balance factory harvester orders against military production; refinery count no longer imposes a three-harvester production cap. Remaining spice, map limits and affordability still constrain the workforce.
- Plan city power for zone growth and recovery after blackouts, and save for nuclear capacity earlier. Nuclear plants now have 750 HP; their cost, power output and explosion are unchanged. Existing saves retain their saved object statistics.
- Reduce AI police funding during a cash shortage after major city losses, then restore it as the economy recovers. Budget commands now correctly affect the issuing player's house.
- Enemy roads can be built over like concrete, but do not extend another house's construction range. Remove road upkeep and separate road maintenance ownership. Redirect completed redundant road orders to another useful gap instead of cancelling them; retain the finished road if no suitable gap is available yet.

Shared QuantBot production changes apply to Dune City and Vanilla where relevant; city tax, zoning, roads and service budgets are specific to Dune City. All multiplayer participants should use the same version.

Validation: 564 tests passed locally; three optional tests skipped. Local dependency audits passed. Remote publication is gated on Linux tests and Windows, macOS and Linux builds.
