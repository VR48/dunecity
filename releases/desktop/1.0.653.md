# Dune City 1.0.653

Play campaign co-op with your friends online! Two commanders can share one house, including its buildings, units and credits, in campaigns and single-mission skirmishes.

- Host from the campaign or skirmish screen with **Host Co-op**, or choose **Multiplayer → Host Campaign Co-op**. Pick the house, starting mission and Internet or LAN hosting. A friend can join through the multiplayer server list; alternatively, choose a QuantBot partner.
- Continue together between campaign missions. Save and resume a shared campaign, or host an existing single-player campaign save. QuantBot co-commanders use normal economic and military planning while respecting mission technology limits.
- Improve early city investment, especially on Brutal: grow spice income faster, respond to harvester unloading queues with more refinery capacity, and build demanded civic services. Police coverage and traffic simulation have been corrected; airports deploy ornithopter patrols.
- Expand onto new rock when construction space runs short. MCV expansion ranks distance from the main base first, with enemy clearance and available room used to choose among suitable nearby sites.
- Improve ornithopter targeting and defence: prefer targets outside rocket-turret and launcher coverage, protect the base and safe harvester engagements, and expand air production when needed. Improve air-defence coverage of exposed buildings.
- Restore original scenario house identities in single-mission games, prioritize QuantBot entries in AI selection, default campaign opponents to QuantBot Easy, and widen player selection boxes to show full names.

See the [multiplayer co-op hosting guide](https://dunelegacy.com/coop.html) for hosting, joining and save/reload instructions.

All multiplayer participants must use the same desktop release and matching mods/configuration. This release uses network protocol 5 and save format 9837; older saves remain readable, while older executables cannot read newly written saves. Browser and Android releases are unchanged.

Validation includes the local test suite, dependency audits, two-instance Internet-server discovery and joining, matching simulation logs, and human testing of two campaign missions plus save/reload. Gameplay connections across separate routers have not been verified in this test. Public packages are gated on Linux tests and successful Windows, macOS and Linux builds.
