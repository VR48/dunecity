# Dune City 1.0.608

Changes since 1.0.601:

- Simplify qBot army control: normal attacks use Hunt, with a loose gathering point near working harvesters and proportional local responses to attacks. Remove the expensive formation controller that could leave armies gathering while nearby buildings were attacked.
- Learn unit production ratios from damage value, kill bonuses and losses across the whole match. Prefer safer factory sites away from enemy forces.
- Expand light factory capacity when funded demand warrants it, and let heavy factories continue filling available army capacity when preferred unit shares are already satisfied.
- Improve harvester safety around launchers, check refinery return corridors and request sufficiently strong nearby clearing teams before missiles hit.
- Allow police reinforcements below 250 military units per house, while retaining qBot military-value and engine limits.
- Rebuild missing roads around surviving city buildings when construction yards have no higher-priority work. Fix completed road placement being incorrectly reported as failed.
- Group mature neighbouring crime outbreaks into a larger force at the worst hotspot. Dangerous occupied buildings contribute one, two or three troopers by density after 4–6 simulation minutes of sustained crime, with a short gathering window and a population gate of 5,000.
- Add regression coverage and telemetry for road completion, launcher safety and grouped outbreaks.

Army, factory and harvester improvements apply to both Vanilla and Dune City. City roads, police and crime changes apply to Dune City.

Older saves remain readable. Multiplayer participants must use the same version. Live balance and performance still need match testing.
