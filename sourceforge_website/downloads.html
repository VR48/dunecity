Build a colony on Arrakis.

Dune City brings city building to Dune II. Zone residential, commercial and industrial districts, connect roads, balance power and taxes, and control crime and pollution while defending your settlement.

Prefer classic strategy? Choose Vanilla mode in the same game to harvest spice, expand your base and command your army. Campaigns, skirmishes, online multiplayer and improved AI are included.
